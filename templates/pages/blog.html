  • Kinematics of a Hexapod Modular Robot

    ABCM Symposium Series in Mechatronics, v. 6, p. 485-496 · 2014

    This article is about the Erekobot Alpha, a modular robot with a hexapod locomotion system built from seventeen identical modules: a spine of five rigid modules plus two per leg. Each leg executes a constant, previously-defined trajectory. The research concluded that the robot is capable of walking forward and back on plain surfaces, and kinematic simulations based on the movement equations were compared against the real robot's leg movements.

    Gonçalves, L. L. C.; Silva Junior, A. R. V.; Torres, A. C. F.; Sousa, A. C. C.; Mota, I. C.; Beggs, A. M.; Koike, C. M. C. C.; Viana, D. M. · Universidade de Brasília, 2014.
