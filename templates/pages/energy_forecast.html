Brazilian Energy Consumption Forecast

An end-to-end AutoML pipeline that trains an AutoML model on historical ONS open data and produces monthly forecasts of electric energy consumption for Brazil's Southeast (SE) subsystem: the country's largest, covering São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Minas Gerais and Espírito Santo. Updated automatically on the 1st of each month via GitHub Actions.
